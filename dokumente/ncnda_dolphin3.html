Article 01
Recitals &
Purpose

SportForYou Srls ("ARS" or "Disclosing Party") is the developer and sole owner of the Dolphin 3 — an autonomous water rescue robot system incorporating proprietary hardware, software, and operational know-how designed to save lives in aquatic emergencies.

ARS desires to disclose certain Confidential Information to the Receiving Party solely for the purpose of evaluating a potential business relationship, partnership, distribution agreement, licensing arrangement, or investment opportunity (collectively, the "Permitted Purpose").

Both parties acknowledge that the Confidential Information is of significant commercial and strategic value, and that its unauthorised disclosure, misuse, or exploitation would cause immediate and irreparable harm to ARS, its business, and its competitive position in the market.

This Agreement sets forth the terms and conditions under which such Confidential Information shall be shared, protected, and — if necessary — returned or destroyed. By signing this Agreement, the Receiving Party confirms it has read, understood, and agrees to be bound by all provisions herein.

Article 02
Definitions

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by ARS to the Receiving Party in any form — oral, written, visual, electronic, or otherwise — including but not limited to:

  • Technical specifications, engineering drawings, CAD data, hardware designs, and prototypes of the Dolphin 3 system
  • Software, firmware, algorithms, source code, control systems, and artificial intelligence models
  • Manufacturing processes, supplier identities, component sourcing, and production costs
  • Business plans, financial projections, pricing strategies, revenue models, and market analysis
  • Intellectual property, patents (pending or granted), trade secrets, trademarks, and know-how
  • Customer lists, distributor networks, partnership agreements, and contact information
  • Any other information designated as confidential by ARS at time of disclosure

Information shall not be deemed Confidential Information if it: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no act or omission of the Receiving Party; (b) was independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of Confidential Information; or (c) was lawfully received from a third party without any restriction on disclosure.

Article 03
Non-Disclosure
Obligations

The Receiving Party agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and specifically undertakes to:

  • Protect the Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care as it uses for its own most sensitive confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care
  • Use the Confidential Information solely for the Permitted Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever
  • Not copy, reproduce, summarise, or create derivative works based on Confidential Information except as strictly necessary for the Permitted Purpose
  • Not reverse engineer, disassemble, decompile, or otherwise attempt to derive the composition, design, or operation of any component of the Dolphin 3 system
  • Not file any patent application, register any design right, or seek any other form of intellectual property protection based on or derived from the Confidential Information
  • Not use the Confidential Information to develop, improve, or support any product or service that competes with the Dolphin 3
These obligations shall remain in full force and effect for a period of five (5) years from the date of this Agreement and shall survive any termination or expiry thereof.

Article 04
Non-
Circumvention

The Receiving Party agrees that it shall not, directly or indirectly, circumvent, avoid, bypass, or obviate ARS's legitimate business interests in any transaction or relationship arising from or connected to this Agreement. Specifically, the Receiving Party shall not:

  • Contact, solicit, deal with, or enter into any agreement with any supplier, manufacturer, technology partner, investor, customer, distributor, or other contact introduced by ARS through or in connection with this Agreement, without ARS's prior written consent
  • Use information received under this Agreement to develop, manufacture, distribute, licence, or market any product or technology that is competitive with or substantially similar to the Dolphin 3
  • Assist, advise, or enable any third party to circumvent the rights and interests of ARS
  • Take any action that would deprive ARS of business opportunities, revenue streams, partnerships, or competitive advantage arising from the Dolphin 3 system or its associated technology
  • Establish any direct business relationship with any party introduced by ARS without ARS's explicit prior written approval
This non-circumvention obligation shall remain in full effect for a period of five (5) years from the date of this Agreement.

Article 05
Permitted
Disclosures

The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to its own employees, officers, directors, legal advisors, and financial advisors who: (a) have a strict and demonstrable need to know the information solely for the Permitted Purpose; and (b) are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those contained in this Agreement. The Receiving Party shall remain fully liable for any breach committed by such persons.

If the Receiving Party is required by applicable law, regulation, or binding court order to disclose any Confidential Information, it shall: (i) provide ARS with prompt prior written notice to the extent legally permitted; (ii) cooperate fully with ARS in seeking an appropriate protective order or other relief; and (iii) disclose only the minimum amount of Confidential Information legally required to comply with such obligation.

06
Article 06
Return &
Destruction

Upon written request by ARS at any time, or automatically upon termination or expiry of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall promptly return to ARS or certifiably destroy all Confidential Information in its possession or control — including all copies, summaries, notes, and derivative works — and confirm such return or destruction in writing within ten (10) business days. Electronically stored Confidential Information must be permanently deleted from all systems, devices, and backup media.

Article 07
Term &
Termination

This Agreement commences on the date of last signature and continues for a period of five (5) years, unless earlier terminated by either party upon thirty (30) days' written notice. All confidentiality and non-circumvention obligations shall survive any termination or expiry for the full five-year period from the date of original execution.

08
Article 08
Remedies

The Receiving Party acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm to ARS for which monetary damages alone would be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, ARS shall be entitled to seek immediate injunctive relief and other equitable remedies without the necessity of proving actual damages or posting a bond, in addition to all other remedies available at law or in equity. The Receiving Party shall indemnify and hold ARS harmless from all losses, costs, damages, and legal fees arising from any breach.
