Right of Withdrawal

If the Buyer is a consumer (a natural person acting outside their trade, business or profession), they have the right to withdraw from this agreement within 14 days of the delivery date, without giving any reason, pursuant to Italian Legislative Decree 206/2005, Arts. 52–58.
